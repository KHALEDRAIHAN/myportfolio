About me

Photographer and cinematographer with a single goal on every job: finding the frame that tells the truth.

Who I am

Khaled Md Raihan

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from the American International Bangladesh (AIUB), and a photographer and cinematographer from Bangladesh with a passion for turning moments into stories. While technology shaped my academic journey, photography became the way I learned to observe the world through light, people, movement, and emotion.

What began as a passion gradually evolved into a journey of experimentation, storytelling, and visual expression. My photographs have been selected for exhibitions including Break the Circle, a collaborative exhibition by IUT Photography Club, BRAC Photography Club, IIUC Photography Club, and NSTU Photography Club, as well as Dorpon. From concerts and events to corporate assignments and creative productions, every experience has taught me to look beyond simply documenting a moment and instead search for the emotion, perspective, and story within it.

My background in Computer Science continues to influence how I create. I bring the same curiosity, problem-solving mindset, and attention to detail from technology into photography and filmmaking. Whether behind a camera or working on a digital project, I'm always looking for the same thing a perspective that feels honest, intentional, and worth remembering.

Philosophy

My approach

01

Intention before execution

Every shot begins with a question: what story does this moment need to tell? I spend as much time thinking as I do shooting.

02

Light is the language

Whether natural or crafted — understanding light is the fundamental skill that separates good photography from great photography.

03

Speed meets precision

In live environments you get one chance. I've trained myself to anticipate moments, not react to them — so the decisive frame is never missed.

04

Consistent visual identity

Every brand, artist and client gets a look that is authentically theirs — developed through collaboration, refined in post-production.
